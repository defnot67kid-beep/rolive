Connect YouTube

To stream to YouTube, you need to:

  1. Have a YouTube channel
  2. Enable live streaming (may take 24 hours for new channels)
  3. Sign in with your Google account that has a YouTube channel

How to get your Stream Key:

1. Go to YouTube Studio
2. Click "Create" → "Go Live"
3. Select "Stream" tab
4. Copy your stream key
5. Use RTMP URL: rtmp://a.rtmp.youtube.com/live2
